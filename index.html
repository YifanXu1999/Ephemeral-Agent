The Ephemeral Paradigm

We believe in a world where intelligence is fluid, scalable, and leaves no residual overhead.

Ephemeralism

Agents are spawned to perform highly specific tasks and killed immediately when they are finished. Infinite scalability with zero idle compute cost.

Persistent Agent State

Agents leave their immutable log, finalize their work securely, and vaporize from the world, leaving behind only the intelligence you need.

Agent Swarm Collaboration

Orchestrate thousands of ephemeral agents simultaneously. They communicate, coordinate, and collapse back into the void when the mission is accomplished.
